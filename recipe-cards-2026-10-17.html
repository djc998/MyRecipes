Monday · Breakfast

High-Protein French Toast with Berries

~32g protein~480 cal20 min

Ingredients

  • 4 slices whole-grain bread
  • 2 eggs + 4 egg whites
  • ½ cup milk · 1 scoop vanilla protein powder (or ¼ cup Greek yogurt)
  • 1 tsp cinnamon · splash of vanilla extract
  • 1 cup fresh berries · maple syrup, to serve

Steps

  1. Whisk eggs, egg whites, milk, protein powder, cinnamon, and vanilla in a shallow dish.
  2. Soak each bread slice 20–30 seconds per side.
  3. Cook on a lightly oiled pan over medium heat, 2–3 min per side, until golden.
  4. Top with berries and a drizzle of maple syrup.

Thursday · Breakfast

Shakshuka with Feta & Chickpeas

~24g protein~420 cal25 min

Ingredients

  • 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
  • 1 onion, diced · 1 bell pepper, diced · 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tsp cumin · 1 tsp paprika · pinch of red pepper flakes
  • 1 can chickpeas, drained
  • 4 eggs · ¼ cup crumbled feta
  • Crusty bread, to serve

Steps

  1. Sauté onion and pepper in olive oil 5 min; add garlic and spices, cook 1 min.
  2. Pour in tomatoes and chickpeas; simmer 8–10 min until thickened.
  3. Make 4 wells, crack an egg into each, cover and cook 5–7 min until whites set.
  4. Scatter feta on top and serve with bread for scooping.

Ham, Egg & Cheese Breakfast Quesadilla

~30g protein~450 cal12 min

Ingredients

  • 1 large flour tortilla
  • 2 eggs, scrambled
  • ½ cup diced ham · ½ cup shredded cheese
  • ¼ cup diced bell pepper · salsa, to serve

Steps

  1. Scramble eggs with diced pepper; set aside.
  2. Lay tortilla in the pan, spread eggs, ham, and cheese over half, and fold.
  3. Cook 2–3 min per side until golden and melted.
  4. Slice into wedges and serve with salsa.

Saturday · Breakfast

Chicken & Egg Breakfast Fried Rice

~30g protein~480 cal15 min

Ingredients

  • 2 cups day-old cooked rice (cold rice fries best)
  • 1 cup shredded rotisserie chicken
  • 2 eggs, beaten · 1 cup frozen peas & carrots
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce · 1 tsp sesame oil · 2 green onions, sliced

Steps

  1. Scramble eggs in a hot oiled pan; remove and set aside.
  2. Stir-fry rice and peas & carrots 3–4 min until hot and lightly crisped.
  3. Add chicken and soy sauce; toss 1–2 min.
  4. Fold in eggs, drizzle with sesame oil, and top with green onions.

Monday · Dinner

Creamy Lemon Pasta with Shrimp

~40g protein~560 cal25 min

Ingredients

  • 1 lb shrimp, peeled & deveined · 8 oz pasta (penne or spaghetti)
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced · zest + juice of 1 lemon
  • ½ cup plain Greek yogurt · ¼ cup grated parmesan
  • 2 cups baby spinach · pinch of red pepper flakes

Steps

  1. Cook pasta; reserve ½ cup pasta water.
  2. Sauté shrimp with garlic 2–3 min per side until pink; remove.
  3. Off the heat, stir yogurt, parmesan, lemon, and a splash of pasta water into the pan; wilt in spinach.
  4. Toss pasta and shrimp in the sauce and serve.

Tuesday · Dinner

Baked Ziti with Turkey Sausage & Ricotta

~40g protein~580 cal45 min

Ingredients

  • 12 oz ziti · 1 lb turkey sausage, casings removed
  • 1 jar (24 oz) marinara sauce · 15 oz ricotta
  • 2 cups baby spinach · 1 cup shredded mozzarella
  • ¼ cup grated parmesan

Steps

  1. Cook ziti 1 min shy of the package time; drain.
  2. Brown sausage, breaking it up; stir in marinara and spinach until wilted.
  3. Mix ziti with sauce and dollops of ricotta; spread in a baking dish.
  4. Top with mozzarella and parmesan; bake at 375°F for 20 min until bubbly.

Thursday · Dinner

Lean Beef Shepherd’s Pie with Cauliflower Mash

~40g protein~480 cal50 min

Ingredients

  • 1½ lb lean ground beef · 1 onion, diced · 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 cups frozen mixed vegetables (peas, carrots, corn)
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste · 1 cup beef broth
  • 1 head cauliflower, broken into florets · 1 tbsp butter · splash of milk
  • ¼ cup grated parmesan

Steps

  1. Steam cauliflower until very tender; mash with butter, milk, and half the parmesan.
  2. Brown beef with onion and garlic; stir in tomato paste, broth, and vegetables; simmer 8 min.
  3. Spread filling in a baking dish, top with cauliflower mash and remaining parmesan.
  4. Bake at 400°F for 15–20 min until golden on top.

Sunday · Dinner

Spinach & Feta Stuffed Chicken Breast

~48g protein~480 cal35 min

Ingredients

  • 2 lb chicken breasts (4 pieces)
  • 2 cups baby spinach, chopped · ½ cup crumbled feta
  • 2 oz cream cheese, softened · 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • Juice of 1 lemon · 1½ lb baby potatoes, halved

Steps

  1. Cut a pocket into each chicken breast. Mix spinach, feta, cream cheese, and garlic; stuff the pockets and secure with toothpicks.
  2. Season chicken; sear 3 min per side in an oiled oven-safe pan.
  3. Add potatoes around the chicken; bake at 400°F for 18–20 min until chicken is cooked through.
  4. Squeeze lemon over everything and serve.

🧊 Weekend Prep Tips

Cook ahead on Saturday/Sunday (about 1–1.5 hours)

  • Cook a big batch of rice and quinoa; portion into containers for the tofu bowls, pesto chicken, and breakfast fried rice.
  • Assemble the baked ziti fully and refrigerate unbaked — it goes straight into the oven on Tuesday.
  • Prep the shepherd’s pie filling and cauliflower mash separately; assemble and bake Thursday.
  • Hard-boil a few extra eggs for snacks and to speed up the cottage cheese toast.
  • Wash and chop all peppers, onions, zucchini, and cabbage; store in sealed containers.

Storage

  • Cooked grains, ziti, shepherd’s pie, and pesto chicken keep 3–4 days refrigerated in airtight containers.
  • Keep slaw, crema, and avocado separate until taco night so nothing goes soggy.
  • Shakshuka sauce (without eggs) freezes well for up to a month.

Reheating

  • Reheat grains and casseroles with a splash of water, covered, so they stay moist.
  • French toast and quesadillas re-crisp best in a dry pan or air fryer, not the microwave.
  • Fish tacos and the stuffed chicken are best eaten fresh — cook those the day of.
