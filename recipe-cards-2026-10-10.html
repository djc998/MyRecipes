Smoked Salmon & Egg Protein Bagel

~32g protein~420 cal15 min

Ingredients

  • 1 everything bagel (high-protein bagel if you can find one)
  • 3 oz smoked salmon
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 tbsp whipped cream cheese
  • 1 tbsp capers · ¼ red onion, thinly sliced
  • Fresh dill · everything bagel seasoning
  • Lemon wedge

Steps

  1. Toast the bagel halves until golden.
  2. Soft-scramble the 2 eggs in a little butter over medium-low heat — pull them just before they look done.
  3. Spread cream cheese on both halves. Layer the eggs, then the smoked salmon.
  4. Top with capers, red onion, dill, a pinch of bagel seasoning, and a squeeze of lemon.

Freezer Turkey Sausage Breakfast Burritos

~26g protein~430 cal35 min

Ingredients

  • 1 lb turkey breakfast sausage (e.g. Jennie-O)
  • 10 large eggs
  • 10 large flour tortillas (high-fiber if available)
  • 1½ cups shredded cheddar
  • 1 red bell pepper, diced · ½ onion, diced
  • Salt & pepper · hot sauce (optional)

Steps

  1. Brown the turkey sausage in a large skillet, breaking it up; add pepper and onion and cook until softened.
  2. Whisk the eggs with salt and pepper and scramble them in the same skillet; fold back in the sausage mixture.
  3. Spoon about ½ cup of filling onto each tortilla, top with cheddar, and roll up tight, tucking the ends.
  4. Wrap each burrito in foil; freeze up to 3 months. Reheat from frozen: microwave 3–4 min (2 min if thawed).

Cottage Cheese Blender Protein Pancakes

~24g protein~380 cal20 min

Ingredients

  • 1 cup cottage cheese
  • 1 cup rolled oats
  • 2 eggs · 1 ripe banana
  • 1 tsp baking powder · 1 tsp vanilla
  • ½ tsp cinnamon
  • Butter or oil for the griddle
  • Maple syrup & berries, to serve

Steps

  1. Blend the cottage cheese, oats, eggs, banana, baking powder, vanilla, and cinnamon until completely smooth. Rest 5 minutes to thicken.
  2. Cook ¼-cup scoops on a buttered griddle over medium heat, 2–3 minutes per side, until golden.
  3. Stack and serve warm with maple syrup and berries. Makes about 8 pancakes (2 servings).

  • 1 can (15 oz) refried pinto beans
  • 1 cup salsa roja

Cottage Cheese Flatbread Breakfast Wrap

~28g protein~350 cal25 min

Ingredients

  • 1 cup cottage cheese
  • 2 eggs
  • ½ tsp garlic powder · ½ tsp Italian seasoning
  • 3 oz sliced deli turkey
  • 2 eggs, scrambled (for the filling)
  • 1 slice provolone · handful of spinach or arugula
  • Tomato slices

Steps

  1. Blend the cottage cheese, 2 eggs, garlic powder, and Italian seasoning until smooth.
  2. Pour onto a parchment-lined sheet pan and spread thin; bake at 375°F for 20–25 minutes until golden and set.
  3. Let cool, then cut into wraps. Fill with turkey, scrambled egg, provolone, greens, and tomato; roll up.
  4. Leftovers keep 3 days in the fridge — great for grab-and-go mornings.

Savory Spinach Oatmeal with Fried Egg

~21g protein~360 cal15 min

Ingredients

  • 1 cup rolled oats
  • 2 cups water or chicken broth
  • 2 big handfuls baby spinach
  • 1 cup sliced mushrooms
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tbsp butter or olive oil
  • 2 tbsp grated parmesan · soy sauce or salt

Steps

  2. Stir in the spinach until wilted; season with soy sauce (or salt) and chili flakes.

  4. Divide into bowls; top each with a fried egg, parmesan, and scallions.

Monday

Light Chicken Tikka Masala

~42g protein~480 cal35 min

Ingredients

  • 1½ lb chicken thighs, cubed
  • ¾ cup plain Greek yogurt, divided
  • 2 tbsp tikka masala spice or curry powder, divided
  • 1 onion, diced · 3 cloves garlic · 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • 1 can (15 oz) tomato sauce
  • ½ cup light coconut milk
  • 1 tbsp oil · cilantro
  • 2 cups cooked basmati rice, to serve

Steps

  1. Marinate the chicken in half the yogurt and half the spices for at least 15 minutes.
  2. Sear the chicken in the oil over high heat until browned; set aside.
  3. Sauté the onion, garlic, and ginger with the remaining spices for 2–3 minutes; add the tomato sauce and simmer 10 minutes.
  4. Stir in the coconut milk and remaining yogurt; return the chicken and simmer 10 minutes.
  5. Serve over basmati rice with fresh cilantro.

One-Pan Turkey Enchilada Skillet

~38g protein~520 cal30 min

Ingredients

  • 1¼ lb ground turkey
  • 1 onion, diced · 1 bell pepper, diced
  • 1 can (15 oz) black beans, drained
  • 1 can (10 oz) red enchilada sauce
  • 6 corn tortillas, torn into pieces
  • 1½ cups shredded Mexican-blend cheese
  • 1 tsp cumin · 1 tsp chili powder
  • Cilantro · lime · Greek yogurt, to serve

Steps

  1. Brown the turkey with the onion, bell pepper, cumin, and chili powder until cooked through.
  2. Stir in the black beans, enchilada sauce, and torn tortillas.
  3. Simmer 8–10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the tortillas soften and soak up the sauce.
  4. Top with cheese, cover, and cook 3 more minutes until melted.
  5. Garnish with cilantro and lime; serve with a dollop of Greek yogurt.

High-Protein Lasagna Soup

~40g protein~480 cal35 min

Ingredients

  • 1¼ lb lean ground turkey
  • 1 onion, diced · 3 cloves garlic
  • 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
  • 4 cups chicken broth
  • 8 oz lasagna noodles, broken into pieces
  • 1 cup cottage cheese
  • ½ cup shredded mozzarella · ¼ cup parmesan
  • 1 tbsp Italian seasoning · fresh basil

Steps

  1. Brown the turkey with the onion, garlic, and Italian seasoning until cooked through.
  2. Add the crushed tomatoes and chicken broth; bring to a boil.
  3. Stir in the broken noodles; simmer 10–12 minutes until tender.
  4. Blend the cottage cheese until smooth; swirl it into the soup with the parmesan.
  5. Serve topped with mozzarella and fresh basil.

High-Protein Chicken Alfredo

~45g protein~560 cal30 min

Ingredients

  • 1 lb chicken breast, sliced
  • 12 oz fettuccine (or protein pasta)
  • 1 cup cottage cheese
  • ½ cup grated parmesan, plus more to serve
  • ½ cup milk · 3 cloves garlic
  • 1 tbsp butter · 1 tsp Italian seasoning
  • 2 cups broccoli florets (optional)
  • Fresh parsley

Steps

  1. Cook the pasta; reserve ½ cup of the pasta water.
  2. Sear the seasoned chicken until golden and cooked through; set aside.
  3. Blend the cottage cheese, milk, parmesan, and garlic until completely smooth.
  4. Melt the butter, warm the sauce gently for 2–3 minutes (don't boil); thin with pasta water as needed.
  5. Toss the pasta, chicken, and broccoli in the sauce; finish with parsley and extra parmesan.

Mediterranean Baked Cod

~38g protein~380 cal25 min

Ingredients

  • 1½ lb cod fillets
  • 1 pint cherry tomatoes, halved
  • ½ cup Kalamata olives · 2 tbsp capers
  • 3 cloves garlic, sliced
  • 3 tbsp olive oil · 1 lemon
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • ¼ cup crumbled feta · fresh parsley
  • Crusty bread or cooked orzo, to serve

Steps

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F.
  2. Toss the tomatoes, olives, capers, garlic, and olive oil in a baking dish; season with oregano, salt, and pepper.
  3. Nestle the cod fillets on top; add lemon slices.
  4. Bake 12–15 minutes until the fish flakes easily.
  5. Top with feta and parsley; serve with bread or orzo to soak up the juices.

Slow Cooker Pork Carnitas Bowls

~40g protein~580 cal15 min + 8 hr slow cook

Ingredients

  • 2½ lb pork shoulder
  • 1 onion, quartered · 4 cloves garlic
  • 1 orange, juiced · 2 limes
  • 1 tbsp cumin · 1 tbsp oregano · 1 tbsp chili powder
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 2 cups cooked rice · 1 can (15 oz) black beans
  • Shredded cabbage · cilantro · salsa

Steps

  1. Rub the pork all over with the spices and salt; place in the slow cooker with the onion, garlic, orange juice, juice of 1 lime, and bay leaves.
  2. Cook on low 8 hours until fall-apart tender.
  3. Shred the pork; spread on a sheet pan and broil 5 minutes for crispy edges.
  4. Build bowls: rice, black beans, carnitas, cabbage, salsa, cilantro, and lime wedges.

🛒 Weekend Prep Tips

  • Saturday cook-up: make the full batch of breakfast burritos and the lasagna soup — both freeze beautifully. Start the carnitas in the slow cooker Sunday morning; it handles itself.
  • Grains in bulk: cook a big pot of basmati rice once (used by the tikka masala, shawarma bowls, and carnitas bowls); portion and refrigerate.
  • Cottage cheese, triple duty: the pancakes, flatbread wrap, alfredo sauce, and lasagna soup all use it — buy a large tub and blend it smooth ahead of time for the sauces.
  • Marinate ahead: get the tikka masala chicken and shawarma chicken into their marinades Saturday night for deeper flavor with zero weekday effort.
  • Storage & reheating: cooked meals keep 3–4 days refrigerated; freeze the rest. Reheat burritos from frozen 3–4 min in the microwave; reheat soups and skillets gently on the stove with a splash of water or broth.
