Monday

Blueberry Cheesecake Protein Overnight Oats

~30g protein~350 cal10 min + overnight

Ingredients

  • ½ cup rolled oats · 1 scoop vanilla protein powder
  • ¾ cup nonfat Greek yogurt · ½ cup milk of choice
  • 1 tbsp chia seeds
  • 1 tsp lemon zest + 1 tbsp lemon juice · 1 tbsp maple syrup
  • 1 cup fresh blueberries, divided
  • 2 tbsp crushed graham crackers, for topping

Steps

  1. Simmer half the blueberries with lemon juice and a splash of water, 5 min, into a compote.
  2. Stir oats, protein powder, yogurt, milk, chia, zest, and maple syrup in a jar.
  3. Swirl the compote through; cover and chill overnight.
  4. Top with remaining blueberries and cracker crumbs; eat cold.

Tuesday

Sheet-Pan Sausage, Egg & Potato Breakfast Bake

~30g protein~420 cal45 min

Ingredients

  • 12 oz turkey breakfast sausage
  • 1 lb baby potatoes, diced · 1 bell pepper, diced
  • 10 eggs · ½ cup shredded cheddar
  • 1 tsp olive oil · salt, pepper, garlic powder

Steps

  1. Roast sausage and potatoes with oil and seasoning on a sheet pan at 400°F for 20 min.
  2. Whisk eggs with salt, pepper, and garlic powder; pour over the sausage and potatoes.
  3. Scatter diced pepper; bake 15–18 min until the eggs are set.
  4. Top with cheddar for the last 3 min; cut into squares — perfect grab-and-go slices.

Thursday

Cottage Cheese Egg Scramble

~28g protein~380 cal10 min

Ingredients

  • 4 eggs · ½ cup low-fat cottage cheese
  • ¼ cup diced bell pepper and onion
  • 1 slice sourdough or a wrap · everything bagel seasoning
  • Butter or cooking spray

Steps

  1. Whisk the eggs with the cottage cheese until combined.
  2. Sauté the veggies in butter for 2 min.
  3. Add eggs; cook low and slow, folding gently, 3–4 min until just set — the cottage cheese makes them extra creamy.
  4. Serve over toast with everything seasoning.

Vanilla Almond Protein Waffles

~24g protein~400 cal15 min

Ingredients

  • 1 scoop vanilla protein powder · ½ cup oat flour
  • 2 eggs · ½ cup Greek yogurt · ¼ cup milk
  • 1 tbsp almond butter · 1 tsp baking powder · cinnamon
  • Berries and maple syrup, to serve

Steps

  1. Whisk everything into a smooth batter.
  2. Cook in a hot waffle iron 3–4 min until golden.

  4. Top with berries and a light drizzle of syrup.

Saturday

Banana Cottage Cheese Muffins

~22g protein (2 muffins)~330 cal30 min

Ingredients

  • 2 ripe bananas · 1 cup cottage cheese · 3 eggs
  • 1½ cups oats, blended into flour
  • ¼ cup honey · 1 tsp baking powder · 1 tsp cinnamon
  • Optional: handful of chocolate chips

Steps

  1. Blend everything until smooth (no protein powder needed).
  2. Fill 12 lined muffin cups.
  3. Bake at 350°F for 20–22 min until a toothpick comes out clean.
  4. Cool; keep in the fridge up to 5 days — great back-to-school-style snack too.

Steak & Egg Breakfast Tacos

~30g protein~520 cal25 min

Ingredients

  • 1 lb flank or sirloin steak
  • 4 eggs · 8 small corn tortillas
  • 1 onion, sliced · ½ cup shredded cheese · salsa
  • Butter · salt, pepper, garlic powder

Steps

  1. Season the steak and sear 4–5 min per side; rest 5 min, then slice thin.
  2. Scramble the eggs soft in butter.
  3. Char the tortillas directly over a gas flame or in a dry pan.
  4. Assemble: steak, eggs, cheese, onion, and salsa in each tortilla.

Monday

Firecracker Chicken Meatballs with Rice

~45g protein~520 cal35 min

Ingredients

  • 2 lb ground chicken · 2 eggs · ½ cup breadcrumbs
  • 4 green onions, sliced
  • Firecracker sauce: ½ cup hot sauce, ¼ cup honey, 2 tbsp brown sugar, 1 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp vinegar
  • Cooked rice, to serve · sesame seeds

Steps

  1. Mix chicken, eggs, breadcrumbs, and green onions; roll into 24 meatballs.
  2. Bake at 400°F for 20–22 min until cooked through.
  3. Simmer the sauce ingredients 3 min until slightly thickened.
  4. Toss meatballs in the sauce; serve over rice with sesame seeds and extra green onions.

Tuesday

Creamy Tuscan Garlic Chicken

~42g protein~560 cal35 min

Ingredients

  • 2 lb chicken breasts · 1 tbsp olive oil · Italian seasoning, salt, pepper
  • 1 onion, diced · 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 cup sun-dried tomatoes, sliced · 2 cups baby spinach
  • 1 cup light cream or half-and-half · ½ cup Parmesan
  • Pasta or crusty bread, to serve

Steps

  1. Season and sear the chicken 5–6 min per side until golden; set aside.
  2. Sauté onion, garlic, and sun-dried tomatoes 3 min.
  3. Add cream and Parmesan; simmer 3 min until thickened.
  4. Wilt in the spinach, return the chicken, and simmer 5 min. Serve over pasta.

Honey Mustard Baked Salmon

~38g protein~480 cal25 min

Ingredients

  • 2 lb salmon fillets (4–5 fillets)
  • 3 tbsp Dijon mustard · 2 tbsp honey · 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced · 1 tbsp lemon juice · paprika
  • Roasted broccoli and potatoes, to serve

Steps

  1. Whisk the mustard, honey, oil, garlic, lemon juice, and paprika into a glaze.
  2. Brush generously over salmon on a lined sheet pan.
  3. Bake at 400°F for 12–15 min.
  4. Broil 2 min for caramelized edges; serve with roasted veg.

Turkey Stuffed Bell Peppers

~40g protein~510 cal45 min

Ingredients

  • 6 bell peppers, halved and seeded · 1.5 lb ground turkey
  • 1 onion, diced · 2 cups cooked rice
  • 1 can black beans, drained · 1 can diced tomatoes with green chiles (Ro-Tel)
  • 1 cup shredded Mexican-style cheese
  • Taco seasoning or elote seasoning

Steps

  1. Bake pepper halves at 375°F for 10 min to soften.
  2. Brown the turkey with onion and seasoning.
  3. Stir in rice, beans, and tomatoes; fill the peppers.
  4. Top with cheese and bake 15 min until melty and bubbling.

Friday

Korean BBQ Beef Bowls

~40g protein~620 cal30 min

Ingredients

  • 2 lb lean ground beef · 4 cups cooked rice
  • 2 cups shelled edamame
  • Sauce: ¼ cup soy sauce, 2 tbsp brown sugar, 1 tbsp sesame oil, 4 minced garlic cloves, 1 tbsp grated ginger, 1 tsp chili flakes
  • 4 green onions, sliced · sesame seeds

Steps

  1. Brown the beef in a hot pan until deeply crusted; drain the fat.
  2. Stir in the sauce and simmer 3 min until thick and glossy.
  3. Cook the edamame; warm the rice.
  4. Bowl it: rice, edamame, saucy beef, green onions, sesame — portion into 4 meal-prep containers.

Saturday

Hearty Lean Beef Chili

~42g protein~540 cal60 min

Ingredients

  • 2.5 lb lean ground beef (93/7) · 2 onions, diced
  • 1 bell pepper, diced · 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tbsp chili powder · 1 tbsp cumin · 1 tbsp smoked paprika
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste · 2 cans crushed tomatoes · 1 cup beef broth
  • 2 cans kidney beans, drained · 1 tbsp cocoa powder (optional, deepens flavor)
  • Greek yogurt and green onions, to top

Steps

  1. Brown the beef in a Dutch oven; drain the fat.
  2. Sauté the veg; add spices and tomato paste, 2 min.
  3. Add tomatoes, broth, beans, and cocoa; simmer 30–40 min.
  4. Top with Greek yogurt and green onions; serve with cornbread.

Sunday

One-Pot Turkey Bolognese

~45g protein~600 cal30 min

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lb ground turkey · 12 oz protein pasta (penne or rigatoni)
  • 1 onion, diced · 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste · 1 can crushed tomatoes · 1 cup broth
  • Italian seasoning · ½ cup Parmesan · fresh basil

Steps

  1. Brown the turkey; add onion and garlic, 3 min.
  2. Stir in tomato paste, tomatoes, broth, and the dry pasta.
  3. Cover and simmer 15 min, stirring, until the pasta is tender.
  4. Stir in Parmesan; top with basil. One pot, zero fuss.

🧊 Weekend Prep Tips

  • Cook once, eat twice: Double the Sheet-Pan Breakfast Bake and the Banana Cottage Cheese Muffins — they cover breakfasts for 4–5 days.
  • Rice & grains: Cook a big batch of rice on Sunday for the firecracker meatballs, Korean BBQ bowls, and stuffed peppers. Cool it quickly and refrigerate; use within 4 days.
  • Sauces ahead: The firecracker sauce and the Korean BBQ sauce keep 5 days in the fridge — whisk them Sunday and weeknights are 15-minute meals.
  • Chili & bolognese: Both taste better the next day. Make a full pot Sunday; portion into containers for easy lunches too.
  • Storage: Meatballs, chili, bolognese, and bowls keep 4 days refrigerated in airtight containers. Freeze anything beyond that for up to 2 months.
  • Reheating: Salmon reheats best gently — 275°F for 10 min or in a covered skillet with a splash of water. Everything else is microwave-friendly; sprinkle water over rice before reheating to keep it fluffy.
  • Overnight oats: Assemble Monday's oats Sunday night in a jar — zero-morning-effort breakfast.
