About Me

Hi — I'm Dad. I cook real food for real life: high-protein meals the family actually eats, on a weeknight schedule, without a sink full of dishes. Each week I share my recipes for the week — sometimes several, and sometimes ones that aren't even in the recipe list yet. These are recipes I've found online that are great quick and easy meals for dads. No fancy equipment, no impossible ingredients. Just dinner, handled.
