About Me

Hi — I'm Dad. I cook real food for real life: high-protein meals the family actually eats, on a weeknight schedule, without a sink full of dishes. Each week I share my recipes for the week — sometimes several, and sometimes ones that aren't even in the recipe list yet. They're high-protein, macro-friendly versions of family favorites — tested and streamlined for a busy weeknight schedule, with no fancy equipment and no impossible ingredients. Just dinner, handled.
