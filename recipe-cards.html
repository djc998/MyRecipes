Monday

Honey Garlic Chicken Stir-Fry

~60g protein~463 cal30 min

Ingredients

  • 4 chicken breasts (about 2 lb), diced
  • 2 lb mixed stir-fry vegetables (broccoli, peppers, snap peas)
  • 3 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce, divided
  • 2 tbsp honey, divided
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced · ½ tsp ginger powder
  • 2 tbsp rice vinegar (or lemon juice) + 1 tbsp cornstarch dissolved in ½ cup cold water
  • Cooked rice or cauliflower rice, to serve

Steps

  1. Spray a hot wok/pan with oil; cook vegetables 10–15 min until tender-crisp. Remove.
  2. Cook diced chicken on medium heat 10–15 min until browned and 165°F inside.
  3. Simmer soy sauce, honey, rice vinegar and the cornstarch slurry 1–2 min until thick and glossy.
  4. Toss chicken and veg in the glaze. Serve over rice.

Tuesday

Turkey Taco Sweet Potato Bowls

~57g protein~539 cal60 min

Ingredients

  • 2 lb lean ground turkey (99/1)
  • 4 medium sweet potatoes · ½ onion · 2 bell peppers
  • 1 packet taco seasoning · 8 tbsp salsa · 2 cups lettuce
  • Chipotle sauce: ¼ cup nonfat Greek yogurt, ¼ cup light mayo, 2 chipotle peppers in adobo, 2 garlic cloves, juice of 1 lime, 2 tsp honey, 1 tsp salt

Steps

  1. Bake pierced sweet potatoes at 400°F for 50–60 min.
  2. Cook turkey with onion and peppers; stir in taco seasoning + splash of water.
  3. Blend all chipotle sauce ingredients until smooth.
  4. Split potatoes; top with turkey, salsa, lettuce, and a heavy drizzle of sauce.

Wednesday

Honey-Glazed Salmon Bowls

~35g protein~490 cal25 min

Ingredients

  • 1 lb salmon, skin removed, cut into cubes
  • Glaze: 1 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce, 1 tbsp honey, 1 tsp rice vinegar, 1 tsp sriracha, ½ tsp garlic powder, ½ tsp sesame oil
  • 2 cups cooked jasmine or sushi rice · 1 cucumber, sliced · 2 green onions
  • Spicy drizzle: 2 tbsp light mayo or Greek yogurt + 1 tsp sriracha + ½ tsp rice vinegar

Steps

  1. Whisk glaze; marinate salmon cubes at least 15 min.
  2. Air-fry at 400°F for 8–10 min, shaking halfway, until caramelized.
  3. Whisk drizzle ingredients, thinning with water as needed.
  4. Bowl it: rice, cucumber, salmon, green onions, drizzle over everything.

Thursday

Greek Chicken & Rice Bowls

~40–52g protein~500 cal35 min + marinating

Ingredients

  • 2 lb boneless chicken thighs
  • Marinade: 2 tbsp Greek yogurt, 1 tbsp olive oil, juice + zest of 1 lemon, 3 minced garlic cloves, 1 tbsp oregano, 1 tsp paprika, salt & pepper
  • Salad: 2 cucumbers, 1 bell pepper, ½ red onion, ½ cup cherry tomatoes, 2 tbsp olives, 1 oz feta, olive oil + lemon
  • Tzatziki: 1 cup Greek yogurt, 1 grated cucumber (squeezed dry), ½ lemon juiced, 1 grated garlic clove, fresh dill
  • Cooked rice, quinoa, or cauliflower rice

Steps

  1. Marinate chicken at least 2 hrs (overnight is best).
  2. Cook in a hot pan 4–5 min per side with lid on, until cooked through.
  3. Chop and toss the salad; stir together the tzatziki.
  4. Assemble: rice, sliced chicken, big scoop of salad, generous dollop of tzatziki.

Friday

Shrimp Fajita Bowls

~35–45g protein~400 cal20 min

Ingredients

  • 1 lb raw shrimp, peeled
  • 1 poblano + 1 red bell pepper, sliced · ½ white onion, sliced · 1 garlic clove, minced
  • ½ tsp smoked paprika · ¼ tsp cayenne · ¼ tsp cumin · ½ tsp garlic powder · splash of tamari/soy sauce
  • 1 lime, juiced · 1 tbsp butter · 1 tbsp avocado oil
  • Cauliflower rice or lettuce cups, guacamole, to serve

Steps

  1. Heat oil in a cast-iron skillet; sauté peppers, onion and spices 5–7 min (add garlic near the end so it doesn't burn).
  2. Raise heat, add tamari and shrimp; cook ~3 min until pink and cooked through.
  3. Finish with butter and lime juice off the heat.
  4. Serve over cauliflower rice or in lettuce cups with guacamole.

Saturday

Spinach & Feta Stuffed Pork Tenderloin

~45g protein30 min

Ingredients

  • 1½ lb pork tenderloin
  • 8 oz fresh spinach, wilted · ½ cup herb feta · ⅓ cup julienned sun-dried tomatoes
  • Pesto, salt, pepper, kitchen twine or toothpicks
  • Green beans + side salad, to serve

Steps

  1. Butterfly the tenderloin lengthwise (don't cut all the way through); pound flat.
  2. Spread a thin layer of pesto, then spinach, feta, and sun-dried tomatoes. Roll tight and tie.
  3. Sear all sides in a hot oven-safe skillet, then roast at 400°F until 145°F inside (~20 min).
  4. Rest 5–10 min, slice, serve with garlicky green beans.

Sunday

Slow-Cooker White Chicken Chili

~53g protein~440 cal10 min prep, 6 hrs slow cook

Ingredients

  • 2 lb boneless, skinless chicken breasts
  • 2 cans cannellini beans, drained · 2 cans Rotel (diced tomatoes + green chiles) · 1 can corn, drained
  • 1 onion, diced · 1½ containers chicken bone broth
  • 1 tbsp cumin · 1 tsp oregano · 2 tsp onion powder · 2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 cup low-fat cottage cheese + 4 oz reduced-fat cream cheese, to finish

Steps

  1. Add everything except the cheeses to the slow cooker. Cook on LOW 6 hrs (or HIGH 3).
  2. Remove chicken; shred with two forks and return to the pot.
  3. Whisk a cup of hot broth into the cottage cheese and cream cheese until smooth; stir in.
  4. Top with cilantro. Leftovers = Monday's lunch.
