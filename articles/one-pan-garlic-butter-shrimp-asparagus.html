One-Pan Garlic Butter Shrimp & Asparagus: The 20-Minute Dinner That Tastes Fancy

Why it works

This is the fastest fancy dinner I know — one pan, twenty minutes, and thirty-eight grams of protein per serving. The shrimp sear in garlic butter until pink and curled, the asparagus stays crisp and bright green, and a squeeze of fresh lemon over the top ties it all together. It tastes like a restaurant plate and cleans up like a weeknight.

The recipe

Ingredients

  • 1 lb shrimp, peeled & deveined
  • 1 bunch asparagus, cut into 2-inch pieces
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced · 3 tbsp butter · 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Juice of ½ lemon · red pepper flakes · rice, to serve

Steps

  1. Sauté asparagus in olive oil 3–4 min until crisp-tender; set aside.
  2. Melt butter in the same pan, add garlic and red pepper flakes, then sear the shrimp 2 min per side until pink.
  3. Return the asparagus, squeeze over the lemon, toss together, and serve over rice.
