Cheesy Garlic Butter Mushroom Stuffed Chicken: Golden Outside, Molten in the Middle

This one came from Easy Recipes by Lila (@easyrecipesby_lila), and Dennis spotted it mid-scroll — a stuffed chicken that actually looks like the photo promises. Chicken breasts get a deep pocket, then a filling of mushrooms sautéed in garlic butter, folded into cream cheese and mozzarella while they're still hot. Season the outside, sear it golden in butter, finish it low — and when you slice it open, the middle is molten. I ran the numbers and a serving lands around 58 grams of protein (my estimate, not hers). Weeknight-easy, restaurant plate.

Why it works

Two things. First, the mushrooms cook down until the pan is nearly dry before they ever meet the cheese, so the filling stays thick and rich instead of leaking out in the skillet. Second, the sear matters: that deeply golden crust is the texture contrast against the creamy middle, and finishing the chicken low and covered keeps the breast juicy while the filling melts through. It's comfort food math — high protein, low fuss, no dry chicken.

The recipe

Ingredients

  • 4 boneless, skinless chicken breasts (about 2 lb / 900g total)
  • 8 oz (225g) mushrooms, sliced
  • 3 tbsp butter, divided
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 4 oz (115g) cream cheese, softened
  • 1 cup (4 oz / 115g) shredded mozzarella
  • 1 tsp Italian seasoning
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste

Steps

  1. Melt 1 tbsp butter in a skillet over medium-high heat. Add the mushrooms and garlic and cook 5–6 minutes until tender, golden, and the pan is nearly dry. Season lightly with salt and pepper.
  2. Stir the hot mushrooms into the cream cheese and mozzarella until creamy and well combined.
  3. Cut a deep pocket into the thick side of each chicken breast, keeping the other sides closed. Spoon the cheesy mushroom filling generously into each pocket.
  4. Rub the outside of each breast with Italian seasoning, salt, and black pepper.
  5. Melt the remaining 2 tbsp butter in the same skillet over medium-high heat. Sear the stuffed breasts 4–5 minutes per side until deeply golden.
  6. Cover and cook on low (or finish in a 375°F / 190°C oven) 10–12 minutes, until the chicken reaches 165°F (74°C) inside. Rest 5 minutes, then serve.
