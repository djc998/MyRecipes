Chicken Fajita Bowls with Cilantro Lime Rice: One Skillet, 44g of Protein, and Dinner in 30 Minutes

Why it works

One hot skillet does the heavy lifting here: the chicken strips brown and the peppers char at the edges at the same time, so the whole savory part of dinner is done in a single pan. While that cooks, the rice gets the treatment that makes the bowl — fresh lime juice and a big handful of cilantro stirred in while it's still steaming. Then it's just assembly: rice, fajita chicken and peppers, black beans, corn, and whatever toppings you're in the mood for. Forty-four grams of protein, thirty minutes, and it meal-preps beautifully — the components hold up in the fridge all week.

The recipe

Ingredients

  • 1½ lb chicken breasts, sliced into strips
  • 3 bell peppers, sliced · 1 onion, sliced · 2 tbsp fajita seasoning
  • 1 cup rice · juice of 1 lime · ¼ cup chopped cilantro
  • 1 can black beans, drained · 1 cup corn
  • Toppings: shredded cheese, sour cream, avocado, lime wedges

Steps

  1. Toss chicken, peppers, and onion with fajita seasoning and cook in a hot oiled skillet until the chicken is cooked through and the vegetables are charred at the edges.
  2. Cook the rice, then stir in lime juice and cilantro.
  3. Build bowls with cilantro lime rice, fajita chicken and peppers, black beans, and corn, then top with cheese, sour cream, and avocado.
