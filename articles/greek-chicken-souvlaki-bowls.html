Greek Chicken Souvlaki Bowls: Charred Lemon Chicken, Cool Tzatziki

Some dinners taste like a holiday on a plate, and this is one of them: charred lemon chicken, cool tzatziki, fluffy rice and crunchy fresh veggies, all piled into one bowl. Forty-four grams of protein, one pan, thirty-five minutes — and it tastes like a Greek summer even in November.

Why it works

Chicken thighs are the whole trick here. They stay juicy where breasts dry out, they take a hard sear without complaining, and cut into chunks they pick up charred edges on every side. The marinade does double duty: lemon juice and oregano season the chicken, and the olive oil helps it brown instead of steam. Then the bowl does the rest — hot rice underneath, cool cucumber and tomato on top, and a big spoonful of tzatziki tying it all together. Every bite gets a little of everything.

The recipe

Ingredients

  • 1½ lb chicken thighs, cut into chunks
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • Juice of 1 lemon · 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp dried oregano · salt & pepper
  • 1 cup rice
  • 1 cucumber, diced · 2 tomatoes, diced
  • ½ red onion, sliced
  • Tzatziki + warm pita, to serve

Steps

  1. Toss chicken with garlic, lemon juice, olive oil, oregano, salt, and pepper; marinate 15 min (or up to overnight).
  2. Grill or pan-sear the chicken in batches until charred at the edges and cooked through. Cook the rice meanwhile.
  3. Build bowls with rice, chicken, cucumber, tomato, and red onion, and serve with tzatziki and warm pita.
