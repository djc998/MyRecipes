Creamy Tuscan Garlic Chicken: One Pan, 42g of Protein, and a Sauce Worth Spooning Over Everything

Why it works

This is a one-pan weeknight dinner that eats like a weekend one: golden seared chicken, a silky garlic-cream sauce with sun-dried tomatoes and spinach, spooned over pasta. About 42g of protein and 560 calories per serving, on the table in 35 minutes — and the whole thing happens in the same skillet, so cleanup stays small.

The recipe

Ingredients

  • 2 lb chicken breasts
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Italian seasoning, salt, pepper
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 cup sun-dried tomatoes, sliced
  • 2 cups baby spinach
  • 1 cup light cream or half-and-half
  • 1/2 cup Parmesan
  • Pasta or crusty bread, to serve

Steps

  1. Season the chicken with Italian seasoning, salt, and pepper.
  2. Sear in olive oil 5-6 min per side until golden; set aside.
  3. Saute onion, garlic, and sun-dried tomatoes in the same pan for 3 min.
  4. Add cream and Parmesan; simmer 3 min until thickened and silky.
  5. Wilt in the spinach, return the chicken, and simmer 5 min.
  6. Serve over pasta with the creamy Tuscan sauce spooned on top.
