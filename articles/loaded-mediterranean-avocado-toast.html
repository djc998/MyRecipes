Loaded Mediterranean Avocado Toast: The 10-Minute Breakfast That Eats Like a Meal

This one came from Anish Khalid (@easy_keto_meal_), and it stopped me mid-scroll for the simplest reason: it's the rare breakfast that looks like it took real effort but doesn't. Thick sourdough toasted till deeply golden, fanned avocado, halved red and yellow cherry tomatoes, a generous crumble of salty feta, roughly chopped walnuts, and — the move that makes it Mediterranean — a slow zigzag of honey over everything. I ran the numbers and a loaded slice lands around 13 grams of protein (my estimate, not his). Ten minutes, no real cooking, and it eats like a meal.

Why it works

It's the sweet-savory thing done right. The feta and flaky salt bring the savory, the honey brings the sweet, and they meet on the creamy avocado so neither one dominates. The walnuts are doing quiet work too — without them it's all soft, and the crunch is what makes you want a second slice. Toast the bread properly (deeply golden, crisp edges) and it holds up under all that weight without going soggy.

The recipe

Ingredients

  • 2 thick slices sourdough or country bread
  • 1 ripe avocado
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, mixed red and yellow, halved
  • 40g feta cheese (about ⅓ cup), crumbled
  • 30g walnuts (about ¼ cup), roughly chopped
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • Flaky sea salt and black pepper
  • Optional: squeeze of lemon, spoonful of dried cranberries

Steps

  1. Toast the bread until deeply golden and crisp around the edges.
  2. Halve the avocado, remove the pit, and slice it thin while still in the skin. Scoop the slices out and fan them across each toast.
  3. Halve the cherry tomatoes and scatter them over the avocado.
  4. Crumble the feta generously over both slices.
  5. Roughly chop the walnuts and scatter them over the top.
  6. Drizzle the honey in a slow zigzag over everything, then season with flaky sea salt and black pepper. Serve right away.
