## Weekend prep plan (Sat/Sun)
1. Cook ~4½ cups dry rice in one batch (Mon/Tue/Thu/Sun dinners).
2. Roast all 8 sweet potatoes at once (breakfast + Friday dinner).
3. Bake the egg bites (Fri) and frittata (Tue) while the oven is hot; bake the Denver omelet bake (Sun) too if there's room.
4. Poach/bake the chicken breast and shred (Thursday fried rice + Friday buffalo chicken).
5. Whisk the chia pudding and store in jars (Saturday breakfast).
