Privacy Policy

Effective: October 22, 2026

About this site

Easy Dad Recipes (easydadrecipes.com) is a recipe website. It shares high-protein family recipes, weekly meal plans, grocery lists, and the stories behind the dishes. This page explains, in plain language, what information the site does and doesn't collect.

What this site collects

The site itself collects no personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-ups, and no forms that store anything about you.

The site uses Google Analytics to understand which pages people visit and how the site is used, so the recipes and pages can keep getting better. That means standard analytics information — such as pages viewed, approximate location, and browser or device type — is collected by Google, and Google Analytics may use cookies. That data is handled under the Google Privacy Policy.

Our YouTube comment tool

The site's owner also uses a private automation tool called "Easy Dad Recipes Comment Poster." It connects to YouTube API Services for one purpose only: posting comments on the owner's own YouTube channel (@easydadrecipes) — for example, posting the recipe ingredients as the first comment when a new video goes live.

The tool runs on the owner's own computer, and its authorization token is stored only on that computer. It collects no information about visitors or anyone else, and it shares nothing with third parties.

Use of YouTube API Services is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
