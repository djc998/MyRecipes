Chocolate PB Protein Overnight Oats

~30g protein~450 cal5 min + overnight

Ingredients

  • ½ cup rolled oats
  • ¾ cup nonfat Greek yogurt
  • ½ cup milk of choice
  • 1 tbsp cocoa powder
  • 1 tbsp powdered peanut butter (PB2) — or 1 tbsp peanut butter
  • 1 tbsp chia seeds
  • 1 tsp honey or maple syrup, pinch of salt
  • ½ banana, sliced + a few strawberries, to top

Steps

  1. Whisk the yogurt, milk, cocoa, PB2, chia, honey, and salt in a jar until smooth.
  2. Stir in the oats, seal, and refrigerate overnight (at least 6 hours).
  3. Top with banana and strawberries in the morning and eat cold.

Tuesday

Sausage & Veggie Egg Muffins

~28g protein~350 cal30 min

Ingredients

  • 8 large eggs
  • 8 oz lean chicken sausage, casings removed
  • 1 cup diced bell peppers, onion & spinach
  • ¼ cup shredded cheddar
  • ½ tsp garlic powder, salt & pepper
  • Cooking spray

Steps

  1. Heat oven to 375°F (190°C). Brown the sausage in a skillet, breaking it up, 5–6 min.
  2. Whisk eggs with garlic powder, salt, and pepper; stir in veggies, sausage, and cheddar.
  3. Spray a 12-cup muffin tin and fill each cup ¾ full (makes ~8 generous muffins).
  4. Bake 18–20 min until set and lightly golden. Eat 2–3 per serving.

Wednesday

Shakshuka with Feta

~22g protein~380 cal25 min

Ingredients

  • 4 large eggs (2 per serving)
  • 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
  • ½ onion, diced · 1 bell pepper, diced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika · ½ tsp cumin · pinch chili flakes
  • ½ cup feta, crumbled
  • 1 tbsp olive oil · fresh parsley or cilantro
  • 2 slices whole-grain bread, toasted, for serving

Steps

  1. Sauté onion and pepper in olive oil 5 min; add garlic and spices, cook 1 min.
  2. Pour in tomatoes, simmer 8–10 min until slightly thickened.
  3. Make 4 wells, crack an egg into each, cover and cook 5–7 min until whites set.
  4. Scatter feta and herbs over the top; serve with toast for scooping.

Thursday

High-Protein French Toast

~35g protein~420 cal15 min

Ingredients

  • 3 slices whole-grain or high-protein bread
  • 3 eggs + 2 egg whites
  • ¼ cup milk of choice
  • 1 scoop vanilla protein powder (or ½ cup Greek yogurt)
  • 1 tsp cinnamon · 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • Berries + sugar-free syrup, to serve

Steps

  1. Whisk eggs, egg whites, milk, protein powder, cinnamon, and vanilla until smooth.
  2. Soak each slice 20–30 seconds per side.
  3. Cook on a buttered or sprayed skillet over medium heat, 2–3 min per side, until golden.
  4. Top with berries and a light drizzle of syrup.

Cajun Chicken Breakfast Quesadilla

~40g protein~550 cal15 min

Ingredients

  • 2 large high-protein or low-carb tortillas
  • 6 oz cooked chicken breast, diced or shredded
  • 3 eggs, scrambled
  • ¼ cup shredded cheddar or pepper jack
  • ½ bell pepper, diced
  • 1–2 tsp Cajun seasoning

Steps

  1. Toss chicken with Cajun seasoning; warm through in a skillet.
  2. Scramble the eggs soft and set aside.
  3. Layer chicken, eggs, peppers, and cheese on one tortilla; top with the second.
  4. Crisp in a dry skillet 2–3 min per side until golden and melty. Eat half now, save half.

Chili Sweet Potato Breakfast Hash

~30g protein~480 cal30 min

Ingredients

  • 1 lb lean ground turkey (93/7)
  • 1 large sweet potato, diced small
  • 1 onion, diced · 1 bell pepper, diced · 2 cloves garlic
  • 1 tbsp chili powder · 1 tsp cumin · 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 can (14 oz) fire-roasted diced tomatoes
  • 1 cup kidney or black beans, drained
  • 4 eggs · 1 tbsp olive oil · cilantro, to finish

Steps

  1. Brown the turkey in a large oven-safe skillet, 6–8 min; set aside.
  2. In the same skillet, sauté sweet potato, onion, and pepper in oil 8–10 min until tender.
  3. Add garlic and spices; stir 1 min. Return turkey, add tomatoes and beans, simmer 5 min.
  4. Make 4 wells, crack in the eggs, cover and cook 5–7 min until whites set. Top with cilantro.

Monday

Lightened-Up Chicken Parmesan

~45g protein~550 cal35 min

Ingredients

  • 2 chicken breasts (about 1 lb), pounded to even thickness
  • ½ cup Italian breadcrumbs or panko
  • ¼ cup grated parmesan, divided
  • 1 egg + 2 egg whites, beaten
  • 1 cup marinara sauce
  • 4 oz part-skim mozzarella, shredded
  • 4 oz whole-wheat or chickpea pasta, cooked, for serving
  • Fresh basil, salt & pepper

Steps

  1. Heat oven to 425°F (220°C). Season chicken with salt and pepper.
  2. Dredge in egg, then in breadcrumbs mixed with half the parmesan. Place on a sprayed sheet pan.
  3. Bake 18–20 min until cooked through (no frying needed).
  4. Spoon marinara over each breast, top with mozzarella and remaining parmesan; bake 5 min more until bubbly. Serve over pasta with basil.

Beef & Broccoli Stir-Fry

~40g protein~520 cal25 min

Ingredients

  • 1 lb flank steak or sirloin, sliced thin against the grain
  • 4 cups broccoli florets
  • ¼ cup low-sodium soy sauce · 2 tbsp oyster sauce
  • 1 tbsp honey · 1 tbsp cornstarch
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced · 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil · 2 cups cooked brown rice, for serving

Steps

  1. Toss beef with cornstarch; sear in a hot wok with half the oil, 2–3 min. Remove.
  2. Stir-fry broccoli with a splash of water until bright green and crisp-tender, 3–4 min.
  3. Add garlic and ginger 30 seconds; return beef.
  4. Pour in soy sauce, oyster sauce, and honey; toss 1–2 min until glossy. Serve over rice.

Turkey Meatballs with Zucchini Noodles

~38g protein~450 cal30 min

Ingredients

  • 1 lb lean ground turkey
  • ¼ cup breadcrumbs · 1 egg · ¼ cup grated parmesan
  • 1 tsp Italian seasoning · 2 cloves garlic, minced · salt & pepper
  • 3 medium zucchini, spiralized
  • 1½ cups marinara sauce

Steps

  1. Mix turkey, breadcrumbs, egg, parmesan, seasoning, and garlic; roll into 16 meatballs.
  2. Bake at 400°F (200°C) 18–20 min, or brown in a skillet.
  3. Simmer meatballs in marinara 5 min.
  4. Sauté zucchini noodles 2–3 min (don't overcook), top with meatballs and sauce.

Thursday

Blackened Fish Tacos

~35g protein~480 cal20 min

Ingredients

  • 1 lb firm white fish (cod, tilapia, or mahi-mahi)
  • 2 tbsp blackening or Cajun seasoning
  • 8 small corn tortillas, warmed
  • 2 cups shredded cabbage slaw
  • ½ cup nonfat Greek yogurt + lime juice, for crema
  • 1 avocado, sliced · lime wedges · cilantro

Steps

  1. Pat fish dry, coat generously with blackening seasoning.
  2. Sear in a hot oiled skillet 3–4 min per side until charred and flaky.
  3. Whisk yogurt with lime juice and a pinch of salt for the crema.
  4. Flake fish into tortillas; top with slaw, avocado, crema, and cilantro.

Egg Roll in a Bowl

~40g protein~450 cal20 min

Ingredients

  • 1 lb lean ground turkey
  • 1 bag (14 oz) coleslaw mix
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced · 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • ¼ cup low-sodium soy sauce · 1 tbsp sesame oil
  • 1–2 tsp sriracha (optional)
  • 3 green onions, sliced · sesame seeds
  • 2 cups cauliflower rice (optional, for serving)

Steps

  1. Brown the turkey in a large skillet, breaking it up, 6–8 min.
  2. Add garlic and ginger; stir 1 min.
  3. Add coleslaw mix and soy sauce; cook 5–6 min until wilted but still crisp.
  4. Stir in sesame oil and sriracha; top with green onions and sesame seeds. Serve over cauliflower rice if you like.

Chicken Sausage & Peppers One-Pan Orzo

~35g protein~560 cal30 min

Ingredients

  • 12 oz chicken sausage, sliced into coins
  • 2 bell peppers, sliced · 1 onion, sliced
  • 1½ cups dry orzo
  • 2½ cups low-sodium chicken broth
  • 2 cups baby spinach
  • ¼ cup grated parmesan · 1 tsp Italian seasoning · olive oil

Steps

  1. Brown sausage coins in an oiled deep skillet, 4–5 min; set aside.
  2. Sauté peppers and onion 5 min; add orzo and Italian seasoning, toast 1 min.
  3. Pour in broth, bring to a simmer, cover and cook 10–12 min, stirring once.
  4. Stir in sausage, spinach, and parmesan until creamy. Rest 2 min and serve.

Cajun Chicken Cottage Cheese Alfredo

~50g protein~600 cal25 min

Ingredients

  • 1 lb chicken breast, sliced into strips
  • 8 oz chickpea or protein pasta
  • 1 cup low-fat cottage cheese
  • ½ cup grated parmesan · ¼ cup milk
  • 2 tsp Cajun seasoning · 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 cups baby spinach · olive oil

Steps

  1. Cook pasta; reserve ½ cup pasta water.
  2. Season chicken with Cajun spice; sear 5–6 min until cooked through. Set aside.
  3. Blend cottage cheese, parmesan, milk, and garlic until silky.
  4. Toss pasta with sauce (thin with pasta water as needed), spinach, and chicken. Serve hot.
