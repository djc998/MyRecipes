# Grocery List — High-Protein Week (Sept 28 – Oct 4)

Consolidated for all 14 recipes (7 breakfasts + 7 dinners). Quantities combined; produce and
proteins are the week-week spenders. Serves roughly 4 people for dinners.

## 🥬 Produce
- 1 pint blueberries
- 4 bananas (ripe — 2 for muffins, rest for smoothie bowls)
- 4 lemons
- 8 large bell peppers (mixed colors — bake, tacos, scramble, chili, stuffed peppers)
- 6 onions (4 yellow + 2 for chili/stuffed peppers)
- 2 heads garlic (~10 cloves total)
- 8 green onions
- 1 lb baby potatoes
- 1 bag baby spinach (2 cups)
- 1 head broccoli (for salmon night)
- 1-inch piece fresh ginger
- 1 sourdough loaf (for the egg scramble)

## 🥩 Meat & Seafood
- 2 lb ground chicken
- 2 lb chicken breasts
- 1.5 lb ground turkey + 1.5 lb ground turkey (bolognese) = 3 lb total
- 12 oz turkey breakfast sausage
- 1 lb flank or sirloin steak
- 2 lb lean ground beef (93/7) + 2.5 lb (chili) = 4.5 lb total
- 2 lb salmon fillets (4–5 fillets)

## 🧀 Dairy & Eggs
- 2½ dozen eggs (~28)
- 1 large tub (32 oz) nonfat Greek yogurt
- 2 tubs (16 oz each) low-fat cottage cheese
- 1 pint milk of choice
- 1 pint light cream or half-and-half
- ½ cup shredded cheddar
- 1 cup shredded Mexican-style cheese
- ½ cup + extra grated Parmesan (one wedge covers it)
- 1 stick butter
- Dijon mustard (check fridge first — you need 3 tbsp)
- Salsa (one jar, for tacos)

## ❄️ Frozen
- 2 cups shelled edamame
- 1 bag frozen berries (backup for smoothie bowls if fresh fruit is short)

## 🫙 Pantry (buy only what you're out of)
- Rolled oats (½ cup + 1½ cups for muffins)
- Oat flour (½ cup — or blend rolled oats)
- Vanilla protein powder (1 scoop) + chocolate protein powder (1 scoop)
- Chia seeds (1 tbsp)
- Maple syrup, honey
- Brown sugar
- Graham crackers
- Granola
- Peanut butter + almond butter
- Chocolate chips (optional, for muffins)
- Small corn tortillas (8)
- Breadcrumbs (½ cup)
- White or brown rice (~6 cups cooked across the week)
- Protein pasta, penne or rigatoni (12 oz)
- 1 can black beans
- 2 cans kidney beans
- 1 can diced tomatoes with green chiles (Ro-Tel)
- 3 cans crushed tomatoes
- 2 tbsp tomato paste (one small can or tube)
- 1 carton beef broth (need ~2 cups)
- Sun-dried tomatoes in oil (1 cup)

## ✅ Check your pantry first (assumed on hand — not listed above)
Olive oil, sesame oil, cooking spray, salt, black pepper, garlic powder, onion powder,
smoked paprika, cumin, chili powder, Italian seasoning, cinnamon, baking powder,
everything bagel seasoning, taco/elote seasoning, soy sauce, hot sauce, white vinegar,
lemon juice, sesame seeds, cocoa powder, cornbread mix (optional, for chili night).
