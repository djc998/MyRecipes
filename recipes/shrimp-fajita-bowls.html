Shrimp Fajita Bowls

~35–45g protein~400 cal20 min

Ingredients

  • 1 lb raw shrimp, peeled
  • 1 poblano + 1 red bell pepper, sliced · ½ white onion, sliced · 1 garlic clove, minced
  • ½ tsp smoked paprika · ¼ tsp cayenne · ¼ tsp cumin · ½ tsp garlic powder · splash of tamari/soy sauce
  • 1 lime, juiced · 1 tbsp butter · 1 tbsp avocado oil
  • Cauliflower rice or lettuce cups, guacamole, to serve

Steps

  1. Heat oil in a cast-iron skillet; sauté peppers, onion and spices 5–7 min (add garlic near the end so it doesn't burn).
  2. Raise heat, add tamari and shrimp; cook ~3 min until pink and cooked through.
  3. Finish with butter and lime juice off the heat.
  4. Serve over cauliflower rice or in lettuce cups with guacamole.
