Apple Cinnamon Cottage Cheese Bowl with Walnuts

~22g protein~360 cal10 min

Ingredients

  • 1 cup cottage cheese
  • 1 apple, diced · 1 tsp cinnamon · 1 tsp maple syrup
  • 2 tbsp chopped walnuts · 2 tbsp granola

Steps

  1. Warm the diced apple in a small pan with cinnamon and maple syrup for 2–3 min, just until softened.
  2. Spoon cottage cheese into a bowl and top with the warm apples.
  3. Finish with walnuts and granola for crunch.
