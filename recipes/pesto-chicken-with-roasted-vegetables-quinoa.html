Pesto Chicken with Roasted Vegetables & Quinoa

~45g protein~560 cal~40g carbs (est.)~23g fat (est.)35 min

Juicy pesto chicken strips roasted with zucchini, peppers, and cherry tomatoes on a single sheet pan, served over fluffy quinoa with a fresh pesto drizzle and crumbled feta — bright, herby, and around 45 grams of protein per serving.

Ingredients

  • 1½ lb chicken breast, cut into strips
  • ½ cup pesto · 2 zucchini, chopped · 2 bell peppers, chopped
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved · 1 cup quinoa, cooked
  • ¼ cup crumbled feta · Salt & black pepper, to taste

Steps

  1. Slice the chicken breasts into strips and lay them on a sheet pan; add the zucchini, bell peppers, and cherry tomatoes, spoon over half the pesto, season with salt and pepper, and mix everything together right on the pan.
  2. Roast at 425°F for 20–22 min until chicken is cooked through.
  3. Serve over quinoa with remaining pesto drizzled on top and feta scattered over.
