Honey Mustard Baked Salmon

~38g protein~480 cal25 min

Ingredients

  • 2 lb salmon fillets (4–5 fillets)
  • 3 tbsp Dijon mustard · 2 tbsp honey · 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced · 1 tbsp lemon juice · paprika
  • Roasted broccoli and potatoes, to serve

Steps

  1. Whisk the mustard, honey, oil, garlic, lemon juice, and paprika into a glaze.
  2. Brush generously over salmon on a lined sheet pan.
  3. Bake at 400°F for 12–15 min.
  4. Broil 2 min for caramelized edges; serve with roasted veg.
