Banana Cottage Cheese Muffins

~22g protein (2 muffins)~330 cal30 min

Ingredients

  • 2 ripe bananas · 1 cup cottage cheese · 3 eggs
  • 1½ cups oats, blended into flour
  • ¼ cup honey · 1 tsp baking powder · 1 tsp cinnamon
  • Optional: handful of chocolate chips

Steps

  1. Blend everything until smooth (no protein powder needed).
  2. Fill 12 lined muffin cups.
  3. Bake at 350°F for 20–22 min until a toothpick comes out clean.
  4. Cool; keep in the fridge up to 5 days — great back-to-school-style snack too.
