Chocolate Peanut Butter Protein Smoothie Bowl

~32g protein~450 cal10 min

Ingredients

  • 1 scoop chocolate protein powder
  • 1 frozen banana · 1 cup frozen berries
  • ¾ cup nonfat Greek yogurt
  • 1 tbsp peanut butter · ½ cup milk (add slowly)
  • Toppings: granola, banana slices, PB drizzle

Steps

  1. Blend everything, adding milk a little at a time — keep it thick and spoonable.
  2. Spoon into a wide bowl (it should hold its shape).
  3. Add toppings and eat immediately.
