Chili Sweet Potato Breakfast Hash

~30g protein~480 cal30 min

Ingredients

  • 1 lb lean ground turkey (93/7)
  • 1 large sweet potato, diced small
  • 1 onion, diced · 1 bell pepper, diced · 2 cloves garlic
  • 1 tbsp chili powder · 1 tsp cumin · 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 can (14 oz) fire-roasted diced tomatoes
  • 1 cup kidney or black beans, drained
  • 4 eggs · 1 tbsp olive oil · cilantro, to finish

Steps

  1. Brown the turkey in a large oven-safe skillet, 6–8 min; set aside.
  2. In the same skillet, sauté sweet potato, onion, and pepper in oil 8–10 min until tender.
  3. Add garlic and spices; stir 1 min. Return turkey, add tomatoes and beans, simmer 5 min.
  4. Make 4 wells, crack in the eggs, cover and cook 5–7 min until whites set. Top with cilantro.
