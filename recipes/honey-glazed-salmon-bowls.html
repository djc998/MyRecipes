Honey-Glazed Salmon Bowls

~35g protein~490 cal25 min

Ingredients

  • 1 lb salmon, skin removed, cut into cubes
  • Glaze: 1 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce, 1 tbsp honey, 1 tsp rice vinegar, 1 tsp sriracha, ½ tsp garlic powder, ½ tsp sesame oil
  • 2 cups cooked jasmine or sushi rice · 1 cucumber, sliced · 2 green onions
  • Spicy drizzle: 2 tbsp light mayo or Greek yogurt + 1 tsp sriracha + ½ tsp rice vinegar

Steps

  1. Whisk glaze; marinate salmon cubes at least 15 min.
  2. Air-fry at 400°F for 8–10 min, shaking halfway, until caramelized.
  3. Whisk drizzle ingredients, thinning with water as needed.
  4. Bowl it: rice, cucumber, salmon, green onions, drizzle over everything.
