Loaded Mediterranean Avocado Toast

~13g protein/slice (est.)10 minMakes 2 slices

Thick sourdough, toasted till deeply golden, piled with fanned avocado, halved red and yellow cherry tomatoes, a generous crumble of salty feta, roughly chopped walnuts, and a slow zigzag of honey over everything. Creamy, salty, sweet, and crunchy on one slice — and a slice lands around 13 grams of protein by our math. Ten minutes, no cooking beyond the toaster, and it eats like a real meal.

Ingredients

  • 2 thick slices sourdough or country bread
  • 1 ripe avocado
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, mixed red and yellow, halved
  • 40g feta cheese (about ⅓ cup), crumbled
  • 30g walnuts (about ¼ cup), roughly chopped
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • Flaky sea salt and black pepper
  • Optional: squeeze of lemon, spoonful of dried cranberries

Steps

  1. Toast it: Toast the bread until deeply golden and crisp around the edges.
  2. Fan the avocado: Halve the avocado, remove the pit, and slice it thin while still in the skin. Scoop the slices out and fan them across each toast.
  3. Scatter the tomatoes: Halve the cherry tomatoes and scatter them over the avocado.
  4. Crumble the feta: Crumble the feta generously over both slices.
  5. Add the crunch: Roughly chop the walnuts and scatter them over the top.
  6. Finish: Drizzle the honey in a slow zigzag over everything, then season with flaky sea salt and black pepper. Serve right away.

Dad tip: slice the avocado while it's still in the skin, then scoop the whole fan out with a spoon — it lands on the toast in one neat row instead of five fiddly pieces.
