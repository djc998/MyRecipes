Firecracker Chicken Meatballs with Rice

~45g protein~520 cal35 min

Ingredients

  • 2 lb ground chicken · 2 eggs · ½ cup breadcrumbs
  • 4 green onions, sliced
  • Firecracker sauce: ½ cup hot sauce, ¼ cup honey, 2 tbsp brown sugar, 1 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp vinegar
  • Cooked rice, to serve · sesame seeds

Steps

  1. Mix chicken, eggs, breadcrumbs, and green onions; roll into 24 meatballs.
  2. Bake at 400°F for 20–22 min until cooked through.
  3. Simmer the sauce ingredients 3 min until slightly thickened.
  4. Toss meatballs in the sauce; serve over rice with sesame seeds and extra green onions.
