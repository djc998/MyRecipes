Tuscan White Bean Soup with Sausage

~21g protein (est.)~470 cal (est.)~27g carbs (est.)~31g fat (est.)40 minServes 6One pot

A rustic one-pot Tuscan soup: Italian sausage browned and crumbled, creamy cannellini beans, and tender kale simmered in a savory chicken broth with garlic, thyme, and a whisper of red pepper flakes. A swirl of cream at the end makes it rich without making it heavy, and a shower of Parmesan and parsley finishes every bowl.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb Italian sausage (mild or spicy)
  • 2 cans cannellini beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1 small onion, diced
  • 2 carrots, diced
  • 2 celery stalks, diced
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 6 cups chicken broth
  • 2 cups chopped kale or spinach
  • 1 tsp Italian seasoning
  • ½ tsp dried thyme
  • ½ tsp red pepper flakes (optional)
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • ½ cup heavy cream (optional)
  • Grated Parmesan and chopped fresh parsley, for serving

Steps

  1. Brown the sausage: Heat the olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add the sausage, breaking it apart as it cooks, and cook until browned and cooked through.
  2. Soften the vegetables: Add the onion, carrot, and celery and sauté for 5–6 minutes, until softened.
  3. Bloom the aromatics: Stir in the garlic, Italian seasoning, thyme, and red pepper flakes and cook for 30 seconds, until fragrant.
  4. Simmer: Add the cannellini beans and chicken broth. Simmer for 15–20 minutes.
  5. Wilt the greens: Stir in the kale or spinach and cook 3–5 minutes, until wilted. Stir in the heavy cream, if using.
  6. Serve: Season with salt and black pepper to taste. Ladle into bowls and top with grated Parmesan and fresh parsley.

Recipe adapted from Zaeem Asad's Tuscan White Bean Soup with Sausage (@easymediterranean.kitchen) — original reel. Macros are estimates computed from the ingredient list (sausage and beans drive the protein); the optional heavy cream is included in the estimates.
