Savory Spinach Oatmeal with Fried Egg

~21g protein~360 cal15 min

Ingredients

  • 1 cup rolled oats
  • 2 cups water or chicken broth
  • 2 big handfuls baby spinach
  • 1 cup sliced mushrooms
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tbsp butter or olive oil
  • 2 tbsp grated parmesan · soy sauce or salt
  • Chili flakes · sliced scallions

Steps

  1. Sauté the mushrooms in the butter until golden. Add the oats and broth; simmer 5 minutes, stirring.
  2. Stir in the spinach until wilted; season with soy sauce (or salt) and chili flakes.
  3. Fry the eggs to your liking.
  4. Divide into bowls; top each with a fried egg, parmesan, and scallions.
