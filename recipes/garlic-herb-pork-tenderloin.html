Garlic Herb Pork Tenderloin

~42g protein~430 cal40 min

Ingredients

  • 2 pork tenderloins (about 2 lb total)
  • 3 tbsp olive oil · 5 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard · 1 tsp each rosemary & thyme
  • 1 lb green beans, trimmed
  • 1 lemon, juiced · salt, pepper

Steps

  1. Heat oven to 425°F. Rub tenderloins with oil, garlic, Dijon, herbs, salt, and pepper.
  2. Sear in a hot oven-safe skillet 3 min per side.
  3. Scatter green beans around the pork; transfer to the oven 15–18 min (145°F internal).
  4. Rest 10 min, slice thin; squeeze lemon over everything.
