Cajun Chicken Breakfast Quesadilla

~40g protein~550 cal15 min

Ingredients

  • 2 large high-protein or low-carb tortillas
  • 6 oz cooked chicken breast, diced or shredded
  • 3 eggs, scrambled
  • ¼ cup shredded cheddar or pepper jack
  • ½ bell pepper, diced
  • 1–2 tsp Cajun seasoning

Steps

  1. Toss chicken with Cajun seasoning; warm through in a skillet.
  2. Scramble the eggs soft and set aside.
  3. Layer chicken, eggs, peppers, and cheese on one tortilla; top with the second.
  4. Crisp in a dry skillet 2–3 min per side until golden and melty. Eat half now, save half.
