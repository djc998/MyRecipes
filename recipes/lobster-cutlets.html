Lobster Cutlets

~26g protein (estimate)~450 cal (estimate)~25 minServes 4

Golden fried lobster tails, butterflied and pounded thin, breaded and fried crisp, served with melted clarified butter. A restaurant dish at home in 25 minutes — crispy outside, sweet and tender inside.

Ingredients

  • 4 lobster tails (5–6 oz each)
  • Salt & pepper
  • ½ cup flour
  • 2 eggs + 3 tbsp milk
  • 1–1½ cups breadcrumbs
  • 1 cup olive oil, for frying
  • Melted clarified butter, for serving

Steps

  1. Prep the tails: Remove the shells, leaving the tails intact. Butterfly each tail and pound thinly.
  2. Season: Season with salt & pepper.
  3. Bread: Coat in flour, then egg beaten with milk, then breadcrumbs.
  4. Fry: Fry in hot olive oil until golden on each side.
  5. Serve: Serve with melted clarified butter.
