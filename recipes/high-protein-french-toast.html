High-Protein French Toast

~35g protein~420 cal15 min

Ingredients

  • 3 slices whole-grain or high-protein bread
  • 3 eggs + 2 egg whites
  • ¼ cup milk of choice
  • 1 scoop vanilla protein powder (or ½ cup Greek yogurt)
  • 1 tsp cinnamon · 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • Berries + sugar-free syrup, to serve

Steps

  1. Whisk eggs, egg whites, milk, protein powder, cinnamon, and vanilla until smooth.
  2. Soak each slice 20–30 seconds per side.
  3. Cook on a buttered or sprayed skillet over medium heat, 2–3 min per side, until golden.
  4. Top with berries and a light drizzle of syrup.
