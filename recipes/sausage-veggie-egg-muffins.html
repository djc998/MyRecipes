Sausage & Veggie Egg Muffins

~28g protein~350 cal30 min

Ingredients

  • 8 large eggs
  • 8 oz lean chicken sausage, casings removed
  • 1 cup diced bell peppers, onion & spinach
  • ¼ cup shredded cheddar
  • ½ tsp garlic powder, salt & pepper
  • Cooking spray

Steps

  1. Heat oven to 375°F (190°C). Brown the sausage in a skillet, breaking it up, 5–6 min.
  2. Whisk eggs with garlic powder, salt, and pepper; stir in veggies, sausage, and cheddar.
  3. Spray a 12-cup muffin tin and fill each cup ¾ full (makes ~8 generous muffins).
  4. Bake 18–20 min until set and lightly golden. Eat 2–3 per serving.
