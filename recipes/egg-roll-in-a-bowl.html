Egg Roll in a Bowl

~40g protein~450 cal20 min

Ingredients

  • 1 lb lean ground turkey
  • 1 bag (14 oz) coleslaw mix
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced · 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • ¼ cup low-sodium soy sauce · 1 tbsp sesame oil
  • 1–2 tsp sriracha (optional)
  • 3 green onions, sliced · sesame seeds
  • 2 cups cauliflower rice (optional, for serving)

Steps

  1. Brown the turkey in a large skillet, breaking it up, 6–8 min.
  2. Add garlic and ginger; stir 1 min.
  3. Add coleslaw mix and soy sauce; cook 5–6 min until wilted but still crisp.
  4. Stir in sesame oil and sriracha; top with green onions and sesame seeds. Serve over cauliflower rice if you like.
