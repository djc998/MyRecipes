Lemon Herb Salmon with Asparagus

~40g protein~480 cal20 min

Ingredients

  • 4 salmon fillets (5–6 oz each)
  • 1 lb asparagus, ends trimmed
  • 3 tbsp olive oil · 2 lemons (1 sliced, 1 juiced)
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced · 2 tsp dried Italian herbs
  • 1 lb baby potatoes, halved · salt, pepper

Steps

  1. Heat oven to 425°F. Toss potatoes with 1 tbsp oil, salt, and pepper; roast 15 min.
  2. Push potatoes aside; add asparagus and salmon. Brush salmon with oil, lemon juice, garlic, and herbs; top with lemon slices.
  3. Roast 10–12 min more until salmon flakes.
  4. Finish with a squeeze of lemon. One pan, done.
