Blackened Tilapia Tacos with Mango Slaw

~36g protein~520 cal25 min

Ingredients

  • 1½ lb tilapia fillets · 2 tbsp blackening seasoning
  • 8 corn tortillas · 1 mango, diced · 2 cups shredded cabbage
  • Juice of 2 limes · ½ cup plain Greek yogurt · 1 avocado, sliced

Steps

  1. Toss mango, cabbage, and half the lime juice for the slaw; stir yogurt with remaining lime juice for the crema.
  2. Season tilapia generously; sear 3 min per side until flaky.
  3. Warm tortillas; fill with fish, slaw, avocado, and a drizzle of crema.
