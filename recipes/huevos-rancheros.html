Huevos Rancheros

~24g protein~450 cal20 min

Ingredients

  • 4 corn tortillas (or tostadas)
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 can (15 oz) refried pinto beans
  • 1 cup salsa roja
  • 1 avocado, sliced
  • ¼ cup crumbled cotija cheese
  • Cilantro · lime wedges · 1 tbsp oil

Steps

  1. Warm the refried beans with a splash of water in a small saucepan until creamy.
  2. Crisp the tortillas in the oiled skillet, about 1 minute per side; keep warm.
  3. Fry the eggs sunny-side up in the same skillet.
  4. Assemble: tortilla, a scoop of beans, one egg, warm salsa, avocado, cotija, cilantro, and a squeeze of lime.
