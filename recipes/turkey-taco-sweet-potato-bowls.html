Turkey Taco Sweet Potato Bowls

~57g protein~539 cal60 min

Ingredients

  • 2 lb lean ground turkey (99/1)
  • 4 medium sweet potatoes · ½ onion · 2 bell peppers
  • 1 packet taco seasoning · 8 tbsp salsa · 2 cups lettuce
  • Chipotle sauce: ¼ cup nonfat Greek yogurt, ¼ cup light mayo, 2 chipotle peppers in adobo, 2 garlic cloves, juice of 1 lime, 2 tsp honey, 1 tsp salt

Steps

  1. Bake pierced sweet potatoes at 400°F for 50–60 min.
  2. Cook turkey with onion and peppers; stir in taco seasoning + splash of water.
  3. Blend all chipotle sauce ingredients until smooth.
  4. Split potatoes; top with turkey, salsa, lettuce, and a heavy drizzle of sauce.
