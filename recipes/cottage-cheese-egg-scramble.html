Cottage Cheese Egg Scramble

~28g protein~380 cal10 min

Ingredients

  • 4 eggs · ½ cup low-fat cottage cheese
  • ¼ cup diced bell pepper and onion
  • 1 slice sourdough or a wrap · everything bagel seasoning
  • Butter or cooking spray

Steps

  1. Whisk the eggs with the cottage cheese until combined.
  2. Sauté the veggies in butter for 2 min.
  3. Add eggs; cook low and slow, folding gently, 3–4 min until just set — the cottage cheese makes them extra creamy.
  4. Serve over toast with everything seasoning.
