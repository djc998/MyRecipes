Creamy Tortellini with Garlic Steak Bites

~51g protein (est.)~1,130 cal (est.)~61g carbs (est.)~74g fat (est.)30 minServes 4

Garlic butter steak bites with a hard sear, folded into pillowy cheese tortellini and a silky Parmesan cream sauce — all built in the same skillet, right in the steak drippings. It eats like a steakhouse splurge and lands about fifty-one grams of protein a serving by our math. Rich, no question — this is the Friday-night one.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb sirloin steak, cut into bite-sized cubes
  • 20 oz cheese tortellini
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 3 tbsp butter
  • 5 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1½ cups heavy cream
  • 1 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1 tsp Italian seasoning
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 2 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped

Steps

  1. Cook the tortellini: Boil the cheese tortellini in well-salted water according to the package directions until tender and pillowy. Drain and set aside.
  2. Season the steak: Pat the sirloin cubes dry and season them all over with the salt and black pepper.
  3. Sear the steak bites: Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over high heat until shimmering. Sear the steak bites 2–3 minutes per side, until deeply browned. Remove to a plate and set aside — keep the drippings in the pan.
  4. Garlic butter: In the same skillet, melt the butter and sauté the minced garlic for about 30 seconds, until fragrant.
  5. Start the sauce: Pour in the heavy cream and bring it to a gentle simmer in the steak drippings.
  6. Finish the sauce: Stir in the grated Parmesan and the Italian seasoning and keep stirring until the sauce is smooth, glossy, and creamy.
  7. Toss the tortellini: Add the cooked tortellini to the skillet and toss until every piece is coated in the sauce.
  8. Return the steak: Slide the steak bites and any resting juices back into the pan and gently fold everything together until warmed through.
  9. Serve: Garnish with the chopped fresh parsley and serve immediately, straight from the skillet or in warm bowls.
