## 🗓️ Weekend Prep Game Plan (Sunday, ~60–75 min)

1. **Overnight oats** — batch 4–5 jars (Mon breakfast + extras). Keeps 4–5 days.
2. **Egg muffins** — bake a full batch of 8 (Tue breakfast). Refrigerate up to 5 days.
3. **Chicken breast** — cook 3 lb simply (bake/poach at 400°F, 20–25 min). Covers Fri quesadilla, Mon chicken parm, Sun alfredo.
4. **Meatballs** — roll and bake 16 turkey meatballs (Wed dinner). Refrigerate or freeze.
5. **Chicken parm breading** — bread Monday's chicken breasts, refrigerate on a tray.
6. **Alfredo sauce** — blend the cottage cheese sauce (Sun dinner). Keeps 4 days.

**Storage rules:** cooked proteins keep 4 days refrigerated; reheat to steaming. Egg muffins reheat 60–90 sec in the microwave. Zucchini noodles cook fresh (2–3 min) — never pre-cook.
