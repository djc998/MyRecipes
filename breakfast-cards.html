Monday

High-Protein Greek Yogurt Bowl

~39g protein~495 cal5 min

Ingredients

  • 1 cup nonfat Greek yogurt
  • 3 tbsp chia seeds
  • ¼ cup milk of choice
  • 1 tbsp pumpkin seeds · 1 tbsp hemp hearts
  • ¼ peach, sliced
  • Stevia drops + 1 tsp honey, to taste

Steps

  1. Add yogurt to a bowl and stir in the milk until creamy.
  2. Mix in chia seeds, pumpkin seeds, and hemp hearts.
  3. Top with peach slices, a drizzle of honey, and stevia drops to taste.
  4. Eat immediately — keeps you full for hours (18g fiber).

Tuesday

Spinach & Feta Omelet

~22g protein~300 cal10 min

Ingredients

  • 2 eggs + 1 egg white
  • 1 tbsp milk · salt, pepper, ¼ tsp oregano
  • 1 cup baby spinach
  • ¼–½ cup feta cheese, crumbled
  • Avocado oil or cooking spray

Steps

  1. Whisk eggs, egg white, milk, salt, pepper, and oregano.
  2. Wilt the spinach in a hot skillet with a splash of water; set aside.
  3. Spray the skillet, pour in eggs, and cook covered over low heat without stirring.
  4. Add spinach and feta to one half, cover until set, then fold and serve.

Wednesday

Thick Berry Protein Smoothie

~25g protein~350 cal5 min

Ingredients (makes 2 servings)

  • 2 cups frozen raspberries
  • 1 cup frozen strawberries · 1 cup frozen blueberries
  • ½–¾ cup milk of choice
  • 2 scoops vanilla protein powder

Steps

  1. Add everything to a high-speed blender.
  2. Blend in short bursts, scraping down the sides as needed, until thick and smooth.
  3. If your blender struggles, let the berries soften 2–3 minutes or add a splash more milk.
  4. Pour and drink — no banana needed.

Thursday

Savory Egg & Cottage Cheese Meal-Prep Bowls

~40g protein~332 cal55 min (makes 4)

Ingredients (makes 4 bowls)

  • 2 cups liquid egg whites · 6 whole eggs
  • 1 cup low-fat cottage cheese
  • ½ tsp garlic powder · 1 tsp salt · black pepper
  • 2 cups fresh spinach · 1 red bell pepper, diced
  • 8 turkey breakfast sausage links, sliced
  • ½ cup low-fat shredded cheddar

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 325°F. Spray 4 oven-safe bowls generously and set on a baking sheet.
  2. Blend egg whites, eggs, cottage cheese, garlic powder, salt, and pepper until completely smooth.
  3. Divide spinach, bell pepper, and sausage across the bowls; pour egg mixture over, filling ¾ full.
  4. Top each with cheddar and bake 45–50 min until fully set. Cool 10 min.
☀️ Weekend-prep win: make 4 on Sunday — they keep 4 days in the fridge. Microwave 60–90 seconds to reheat.

Friday

Turkey Breakfast Burritos (Meal Prep)

~53g protein~396 cal40 min (makes 8)

Ingredients (makes 8 burritos)

  • 2 lb lean ground turkey (99/1)
  • 8 eggs · 1 cup egg whites
  • 1 pack bacon bits · 2 cups reduced-fat cheese
  • 8 low-carb burrito tortillas
  • 2 tsp each: onion powder, garlic powder, salt, pepper
  • Buffalo sauce: ½ cup nonfat Greek yogurt, 2 tbsp light mayo, 2 tbsp buffalo sauce, juice of 1 lime, ½ tsp each paprika, garlic & onion powder

Steps

  1. Brown the seasoned ground turkey; set aside. Soft-scramble the eggs and egg whites.
  2. Whisk all buffalo sauce ingredients until smooth.
  3. Combine turkey, bacon bits, cheese, and sauce with the eggs.
  4. Fill tortillas, fold tight, and pan-toast until golden. Wrap and store.
☀️ Weekend-prep win: make all 8 on Sunday — freeze them, then bake 15–20 min at 350°F or air-fry straight from frozen.

Saturday

3-Ingredient Protein Pancakes

~30g protein~300 cal (base)15 min

Ingredients

  • ½ cup cottage cheese
  • ½ cup oats
  • ½ cup egg whites
  • 1 tsp baking powder · cinnamon · pinch of salt
  • Toppings: blueberries, peanut butter, sugar-free syrup

Steps

  1. Blend everything (except toppings) until smooth and creamy; let the batter rest 5 minutes to thicken.
  2. Heat a non-stick pan over medium-low; lightly grease if needed.
  3. Pour small-to-medium pancakes; cook until bubbles form, then flip 1–2 min until golden.
  4. Stack and top with blueberries, peanut butter, and sugar-free syrup.

Sunday

Smoked Salmon Scrambled Eggs

~30g protein~400 cal10 min

Ingredients

  • 4 eggs
  • 3–4 oz smoked salmon, diced
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • ¼ tsp everything bagel seasoning, plus more to garnish
  • Minced parsley (optional) · toast, to serve

Steps

  1. Whisk eggs with everything bagel seasoning until light and frothy.
  2. Melt butter in a non-stick pan over medium-low; pour in eggs.
  3. Drag a spatula across the pan slowly so big, soft curds form.
  4. When halfway cooked, fold in the salmon for 15–20 seconds until eggs are just set but still glossy. Serve on toast with parsley.
