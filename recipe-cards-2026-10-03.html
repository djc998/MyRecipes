Monday

Cinnamon Roll Protein Baked Oats

~25g protein~380 cal30 min

Ingredients

  • 1 cup rolled oats · 1 scoop vanilla protein powder
  • ½ cup low-fat cottage cheese · ½ cup milk of choice
  • 1 egg · 1 tsp baking powder · 1 tbsp cinnamon, divided
  • 2 tbsp maple syrup · 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 2 tbsp cream cheese, softened · 1 tbsp milk (for the glaze)

Steps

  1. Heat oven to 375°F. Blend oats, protein powder, cottage cheese, milk, egg, baking powder, 2 tsp cinnamon, maple syrup, and vanilla until smooth.
  2. Pour into a greased 8-inch baking dish; swirl in a cinnamon-sugar ripple with a knife.
  3. Bake 22–25 min until puffed and set in the center.
  4. Whisk cream cheese with milk and drizzle over the top. Cut into 4 squares.

Tuesday

Mediterranean Egg White Frittata

~24g protein~280 cal35 min

Ingredients

  • 2 cups liquid egg whites · ½ cup low-fat cottage cheese
  • 1 cup baby spinach · ½ cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • ¼ red onion, diced · ¼ cup crumbled feta
  • 1 tsp olive oil · salt, pepper, dried oregano

Steps

  1. Heat oven to 375°F. Sauté onion in olive oil in an oven-safe skillet 3 min; add spinach until wilted.
  2. Whisk egg whites with cottage cheese, salt, pepper, and oregano; pour over the veggies.
  3. Scatter tomatoes and feta on top; bake 20–25 min until set and lightly golden.
  4. Rest 5 min, slice into wedges. Great reheated for 2–3 days.

Greek Yogurt Power Parfait with Almond Butter

~28g protein~420 cal5 min

Ingredients

  • 1½ cups nonfat Greek yogurt · 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 tbsp almond butter, melted slightly

Steps

  1. Stir yogurt with half the honey.

Thursday

Loaded Breakfast Sweet Potatoes

~26g protein~440 cal50 min

Ingredients

  • 4 medium sweet potatoes
  • 12 oz turkey breakfast sausage
  • 8 eggs, scrambled · ½ cup shredded cheddar
  • 2 green onions, sliced · 1 tsp olive oil · salt, pepper

Steps

  1. Heat oven to 400°F. Prick sweet potatoes, rub with olive oil and salt; roast 40–45 min until tender.
  2. Meanwhile, brown the sausage and cook the eggs; keep both warm.
  3. Split each potato open and fluff the flesh with a fork.
  4. Stuff with scrambled eggs and sausage; top with cheddar and green onions. Wrap leftovers individually — they reheat well.

Friday

Spinach & Feta Protein Egg Bites

~22g protein~320 cal35 min (12 bites)

Ingredients

  • 10 eggs · 1 cup low-fat cottage cheese
  • 1 cup fresh spinach, chopped · ⅓ cup crumbled feta
  • ¼ cup diced red bell pepper
  • Cooking spray · salt, pepper, garlic powder

Steps

  1. Heat oven to 350°F. Blend eggs, cottage cheese, salt, pepper, and garlic powder until smooth.
  2. Grease a 12-cup muffin tin; divide spinach, feta, and pepper among cups.
  3. Pour batter over fillings; bake 22–25 min until set.
  4. Cool 5 min, release with a knife. Grab-and-go for days — refrigerate up to 5 days.

Saturday

Chocolate Chia Protein Pudding

~25g protein~360 cal10 min + chill

Ingredients

  • 3 tbsp chia seeds · ¾ cup milk of choice
  • ¾ cup nonfat Greek yogurt
  • 1 scoop chocolate protein powder · 1 tbsp cocoa powder
  • 1 tbsp maple syrup · toppings: berries, crushed peanuts

Steps

  1. Whisk chia, milk, yogurt, protein powder, cocoa, and maple syrup in a bowl.
  2. Rest 5 min, whisk again to break up clumps.
  3. Cover and chill at least 2 hours (overnight is best). Makes 2 jars.
  4. Top with berries and peanuts before serving.

Sunday

Denver Omelet Bake

~27g protein~390 cal35 min

Ingredients

  • 10 eggs · ¼ cup milk · ¼ cup low-fat cottage cheese
  • 6 oz deli ham, diced
  • 1 green bell pepper, diced · ½ onion, diced
  • ½ cup shredded cheddar · salt, pepper

Steps

  1. Heat oven to 375°F. Spread ham, pepper, and onion in a greased 9×13 sheet pan.
  2. Whisk eggs, milk, cottage cheese, salt, and pepper; pour over the pan filling.
  3. Bake 22–25 min until set; scatter cheddar for the last 3 min.
  4. Slice into squares — eats well as-is or wrapped in tortillas.

Honey Garlic Chicken Thighs with Rice

~42g protein~620 cal25 min

Ingredients

  • 2 lb boneless, skinless chicken thighs
  • ⅓ cup honey · ¼ cup low-sodium soy sauce
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced · 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
  • 2 tsp cornstarch + 2 tbsp water
  • 4 cups cooked jasmine rice · sliced scallions

Steps

  1. Sear chicken in a hot oiled skillet, 5–6 min per side; set aside.
  2. In the same pan, simmer honey, soy sauce, garlic, and vinegar 3 min.
  3. Stir in the cornstarch slurry; return chicken and coat in the glossy sauce, 2 min.
  4. Serve over rice; top with scallions.

Tuesday

Lightened-Up Mongolian Beef

~40g protein~560 cal30 min

Ingredients

  • 1½ lb flank steak, thinly sliced
  • ⅓ cup low-sodium soy sauce · ⅓ cup brown sugar (or honey)
  • 1 tbsp grated ginger · 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tbsp cornstarch · 4 green onions, sliced
  • 3 cups cooked white or brown rice

Steps

  1. Toss beef with cornstarch; sear in a hot oiled skillet in batches, 2–3 min per side.
  2. Remove beef; sauté ginger and garlic 1 min.
  3. Add soy sauce and brown sugar; simmer 3 min until thick and syrupy.
  4. Return beef to the pan, toss to coat; serve over rice with green onions.

Lemon Herb Salmon with Asparagus

~40g protein~480 cal20 min

Ingredients

  • 4 salmon fillets (5–6 oz each)
  • 1 lb asparagus, ends trimmed
  • 3 tbsp olive oil · 2 lemons (1 sliced, 1 juiced)
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced · 2 tsp dried Italian herbs
  • 1 lb baby potatoes, halved · salt, pepper

Steps

  1. Heat oven to 425°F. Toss potatoes with 1 tbsp oil, salt, and pepper; roast 15 min.
  2. Push potatoes aside; add asparagus and salmon. Brush salmon with oil, lemon juice, garlic, and herbs; top with lemon slices.
  3. Roast 10–12 min more until salmon flakes.
  4. Finish with a squeeze of lemon. One pan, done.

High-Protein Chicken Fried Rice

~38g protein~590 cal25 min

Ingredients

  • 1½ lb chicken breast, cubed
  • 4 cups cold cooked rice (day-old is best)
  • 4 eggs, scrambled · 2 cups frozen peas & carrots
  • 3 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce · 1 tbsp sesame oil
  • 3 green onions, sliced · 2 cloves garlic, minced

Steps

  1. Cook chicken in a hot oiled wok or skillet until browned; set aside.
  2. Scramble eggs in the same pan; add garlic and frozen peas & carrots, 2 min.
  3. Add rice; press into the pan and let it crisp 3–4 min, then stir.
  4. Add soy sauce, sesame oil, and chicken; toss 2 min. Top with green onions.

Garlic Herb Pork Tenderloin

~42g protein~430 cal40 min

Ingredients

  • 2 pork tenderloins (about 2 lb total)
  • 3 tbsp olive oil · 5 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard · 1 tsp each rosemary & thyme
  • 1 lb green beans, trimmed
  • 1 lemon, juiced · salt, pepper

Steps

  1. Heat oven to 425°F. Rub tenderloins with oil, garlic, Dijon, herbs, salt, and pepper.
  2. Sear in a hot oven-safe skillet 3 min per side.
  3. Scatter green beans around the pork; transfer to the oven 15–18 min (145°F internal).
  4. Rest 10 min, slice thin; squeeze lemon over everything.

Shrimp Fajita Bowls

~36g protein~520 cal25 min

Ingredients

  • 1½ lb large shrimp, peeled
  • 2 bell peppers, sliced · 1 large onion, sliced
  • 2 tbsp fajita seasoning · 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 3 cups cooked cilantro-lime rice
  • Toppings: avocado, salsa, Greek yogurt, lime wedges

Steps

  1. Toss shrimp, peppers, and onion with oil and fajita seasoning.
  2. Sear peppers and onion in a hot skillet 5 min until charred at the edges.
  3. Add shrimp; cook 2–3 min until pink and curled.
  4. Serve over cilantro-lime rice with avocado, salsa, a dollop of Greek yogurt, and lime.

🛒 Weekend Prep Tips

Cook ahead on Saturday/Sunday (about 1–1.5 hours)

  • Sunday morning: Roast the sweet potatoes (Thu dinner + Thu breakfast in one batch) and poach the chicken for Friday dinner. Bake the egg bites (Fri breakfast) and the frittata (Tue) while the oven is hot.
  • Overnight staples: Mix the chia pudding (Sat) and prep the cinnamon roll baked oats batter the night before — bake fresh in the morning.
  • Rice base: Cook a big batch of rice once (Mon + Thu + Sun dinners + Sun breakfast burrito-style bowls) — day-old rice fries best for Thursday's fried rice.
  • Egg batches: The Denver omelet bake (Sun) and frittata (Tue) both reheat beautifully; cut into portions and refrigerate.

Storage

  • Refrigerate cooked components in sealed containers: most last 3–4 days (egg bites up to 5).
  • Keep toppings (avocado, yogurt, salsa, fresh herbs) separate until serving.

Reheating

  • Reheat egg dishes at 350°F for 10–12 min (covered) or microwave 60–90 sec — don't blast them.
  • Stir-fries, fried rice, and shrimp bowls: hot skillet 3–4 min with a splash of water to steam back to life.
  • Pork tenderloin and chicken: warm gently — 325°F covered — so the protein doesn't dry out.
